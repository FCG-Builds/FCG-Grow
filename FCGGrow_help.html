First Time Setup

Get FCG Grow running in under 5 minutes

When you open FCG Grow for the first time a setup screen appears. Here is exactly what to do:

1

Enter your organization name

This is just what you call your business. "Fairy Circle Garden", "Justin's Resale", "My Food Truck" -- anything works. This is what shows up in the app and what the AI calls you.

2

NAICS Code skip if unsure

Completely optional. If you resell products, freelance, or just aren't sure -- leave it blank. The AI works great without it. Type a keyword or number in the field to filter the list. What is a NAICS code?

3

Business Type optional

Plain English is fine. "I resell vintage electronics", "landscaping company", "food truck", "freelance designer". The more specific you are the better the AI advice gets.

4

Top Goals optional

What are you working toward? "Get my first 10 clients", "win a government contract", "hit $100K revenue". The AI factors these into every recommendation it gives you.

5

Add an AI Provider

This is how FCG Grow talks to an AI. You need at least one. If you have no budget, pick OpenRouter -- it is completely free and has 300+ models including thinking models. Step-by-step guide to getting a free key.

6

Hit Save and Launch

Your board opens. You are ready. The AI panel is on the right -- say hello and ask it what you should work on today.

Need to change anything? Click the ⚙ cogwheel in the top right bar at any time to reopen Settings. Nothing is permanent except deleting tasks.

What is FCG Grow?

FCG Grow is a task management board with a built-in AI assistant that actually knows your business. It helps you stay organized, figure out what to work on, and get things done -- without needing a big team or expensive software.

You do not need to be technical to use it. If you can type a message you can use FCG Grow.

It is completely free. Open source. No subscription. No ads. Yours to keep.

The one-sentence version: Your board holds all your tasks. The AI on the right reads your board and helps you manage it, write code, answer questions, and run your business -- using plain English.

Interface Overview

Here is what every part of the screen does:

Top Bar

FCG Grow

File / View / Tools
Menu buttons. File has import/export. Tools has calendar import and other utilities.
⚙
Opens Settings. Change your org name, NAICS, goals, and AI providers here.
AI
Toggles the AI chat panel open and closed.
+ Add task
Opens the Add Task dialog. Fill in title, status, priority, category, due date, and notes.

Workspace Tabs

The small tab row just below the logo shows your workspaces. The active one is highlighted in green. Click + to create a new workspace. Click ✕ to delete the current one (only visible when you have more than one).

Stats Bar

Shows Total tasks, To Do count, In Progress count, Active (non-done), and Done count. Updates instantly as you move tasks.

Filter & Search Bar

The filter bar shows all categories as lime green labels. The active filter has a lime green background with dark text. Inactive filters show lime text on a dark background — still clearly lime, just not selected. Click any label to filter. Search box filters in real time as you type.

Board Columns

Four columns: To Do, In Progress, Review, Done. Each shows a count badge. Scroll within any column independently.

AI Chat Panel

On the right side. Shows the active model name in the top right corner. Type in the box at the bottom and hit Send. Clear resets the conversation history.

Using the Board

Your board has four columns. Every task lives in one of them:

  • To Do — Things you have not started yet
  • In Progress — Things you are actively working on right now
  • Review — Done but needs a check before closing out
  • Done — Finished. Celebrate.

Adding a Task

  • Click + Add task in the top right corner
  • Tell the AI: "Add a task to call my insurance broker by Friday" — it appears instantly

Editing a Task

Click Edit on any card to open the edit dialog. You can change the title, status, priority, category, due date, and notes. Click Save task when done.

Moving a Task

  • Use the Move to... dropdown on the card — pick any column
  • Drag the card to another column (see Drag & Drop below)
  • Tell the AI: "Move [task name] to In Progress"

Reassigning a Category

Click the category badge on any card (e.g. Operations) to open a quick category picker. Choose any category from the list and the task updates instantly — no dialog required.

Clicking a Card

A single click on a card (not on a button) opens the Edit dialog for that task. Same as clicking Edit.

Deleting a Task

Click Del on any card. You will be asked to confirm. This cannot be undone -- export your tasks first if you want a backup.

Priorities and Due Dates

Tasks with a past due date show the date in red. The AI reads priority and due date when recommending what to work on today. High priority tasks appear with a red accent on the left edge of the card.

Drag & Drop

You can drag any task card from any column to any other column directly with your mouse.

1
Click and hold on any task card and immediately start moving — no long press required
2
A ghost label of the task title appears under your cursor as you drag
3
Release over any column to drop the card there
4
The board updates instantly — only the affected columns refresh, not the entire board
Speed note: FCG Grow only redraws the two columns involved in the move. With 200+ tasks on the board it stays snappy because it is not rebuilding every card every time.

If you accidentally drag to the wrong column just use the Move to... dropdown to correct it, or ask the AI to move it back.

Filters & Search

Category Filters

The filter bar shows a button for each category. The active filter is highlighted lime green with dark text — same style as the Send button. Inactive filters are dark with muted text.

Click any category to show only tasks in that category across all columns. Click All to return to the full board view.

Available categories: Grant, Operations, Admin, Outreach, Marketing, Finance, Other

The search box on the right of the filter bar filters in real time as you type. It searches task titles, notes, and categories simultaneously. Clear the search box to return to the full view.

Calendar Import

What is an ICS file?

ICS is a standard file format that calendars use to share events. When you import a calendar into FCG Grow it reads this format and turns your events into tasks on your board.

Important: Once imported your tasks are completely independent from your calendar. Changes you make on the board do not affect your calendar and calendar changes do not affect your board. It is a one-time copy, not an ongoing sync.

Importing from Google Calendar

1
Go to calendar.google.com
2
Click the gear icon (Settings) in the top right
3
In the left sidebar click your calendar name under My calendars
4
Scroll down to Integrate calendar
5
Copy the Secret address in iCal format — this is your ICS URL
6
In FCG Grow go to Tools → Import calendar, paste the URL, click Import from URL

Importing from Outlook

1
Go to outlook.com and open Calendar
2
Click the gear icon, then View all Outlook settings
3
Go to Calendar → Shared calendars
4
Under Publish a calendar, select your calendar and click Publish
5
Copy the ICS link and paste into FCG Grow Tools → Import calendar

Importing a .ics File

If you have a .ics file on your computer, go to Tools → Import calendar then click Load .ics file and select your file.

Tip: After importing ask the AI to clean things up — "Remove all tasks older than today" or "Delete anything that looks personal, keep only business tasks".

Thinking Models

Some AI models (called "thinking" or "reasoning" models) spend time working through a problem internally before responding. They produce significantly better results for complex tasks but take longer than standard models.

Model TypeSpeedBest ForMax Tokens
Standard (Haiku, Llama, Mistral)Fast (1–5s)Quick tasks, board ops, simple questions4,096
Thinking (Trinity, DeepSeek-R1, o1)Slower (10–60s)Complex strategy, code, multi-step plans16,000

FCG Grow automatically detects thinking models by name and gives them 16,000 token output space so their reasoning has room to run. You do not need to configure anything.

Recommended Free Thinking Models (via OpenRouter)

  • arcee-ai/trinity-large-thinking — Excellent for complex business strategy
  • deepseek/deepseek-r1:free — Strong reasoning, reliable free tier
  • deepseek/deepseek-chat:free — Fast, good balance of speed and quality
Best workflow: Use a fast model (Haiku, Llama) for daily board management. Switch to a thinking model when you need a real strategy, complex code, or a detailed plan.

What is a NAICS Code?

NAICS stands for North American Industry Classification System

It is a 6-digit number the United States, Canada, and Mexico use to categorize every type of business by what it does. The government created it so businesses, economists, and agencies can all speak the same language when talking about industries.

FCG Grow uses your NAICS code to give you advice specific to your field. A landscaping company gets different advice than a restaurant. The more specific the code the more tailored the guidance.

You do not need a NAICS code to use FCG Grow. Skip it if you are unsure -- the AI works great without it. You can always add it later in Settings (⚙).

How to Find Your NAICS Code

In the Settings NAICS field, just type a keyword like "landscaping" or "resale" and a filtered list will appear. Or visit census.gov/naics and search by keyword.

Workspaces

Each workspace is a completely separate board for a different business or project. Tasks, settings, and AI context are fully isolated between workspaces.

The workspace tabs sit inside the top bar just below the logo. The active workspace tab is highlighted green. Click a tab to switch instantly. Click the + button to add a new workspace. The ✕ button deletes the current workspace (only visible when you have more than one).

FCG Grow remembers which workspace you had open and reopens it automatically next time you launch.

Note: Deleting a workspace permanently deletes all its tasks. Export your tasks first using File → Export Tasks if you want a backup.

Managing AI Providers

Each provider card shows the provider name, API key field, and model selector. The order matters — provider 1 is tried first, then 2, then 3 if the previous ones fail.

  • Click Refresh model list to fetch the latest available models from that provider
  • Click + Add Provider to add a second or third backup provider
  • The current active model is shown in the top right corner of the AI panel
Thinking models: FCG Grow automatically detects thinking/reasoning models and gives them 16,000 output tokens instead of 4,096. No configuration needed — just select the model.

Power Tips

  • Use specific task names. "Follow up with Dallas VA re: grounds maintenance RFP" is far more useful than "Follow up VA".
  • Add due dates to everything important. The AI flags overdue items automatically and factors urgency into its daily recommendations.
  • After a calendar import, ask the AI to clean it up: "Remove all tasks older than today and delete anything that looks personal".
  • Export your tasks regularly as a backup using File → Export Tasks. It saves as a JSON file you can reimport.
  • Add multiple providers. OpenRouter free as #1 means you almost never burn paid credits, but Claude is there as backup for complex questions.
  • The Clear button in the AI panel resets the conversation history. Use it when switching topics to get cleaner, faster responses.
  • For complex multi-step tasks (bulk categorization, strategy, code), use a thinking model. For quick daily operations use a fast model.
  • The AI reads your Goals field every single message. Keep it updated as your priorities change.
  • You can ask the AI to write code, draft documents, or answer general questions -- it is not limited to board management.
  • Click any category badge on a card to instantly reassign it — faster than opening Edit.
  • Drag cards between any column — pick up from To Do, In Progress, Review, or Done and drop anywhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

My board is empty after moving the app to a new folder.

Your tasks are saved in a fcggrow_workspaces/ folder next to the app. Copy that entire folder to the new location and your tasks will reappear.

The AI says "All providers failed" or gives a 404/401 error.

Your API key is wrong, expired, or the model is unavailable. Go to ⚙ Settings, find your provider card, clear the key field and paste a fresh one. For 404 errors the model may have been removed from the provider — click Refresh model list and pick an active one.

I asked the AI to do something complex and it did nothing.

The model likely ran out of output tokens trying to reason through the request. Switch to a thinking model (like deepseek/deepseek-r1:free) which gets 16,000 token output space. Also try breaking the request into smaller steps.

The AI used to refuse to write code or answer non-business questions.

Fixed in the current version. FCG Grow now explicitly tells the AI it can help with any topic including coding, technical questions, writing, and general advice -- not just business operations.

Does my data go to the cloud or get shared with anyone?

No. Your tasks and settings stay on your computer in the FCG Grow folder. The only data that leaves your machine is the message you send to the AI provider -- same as using their website directly. If you use Ollama (local), absolutely nothing leaves your machine.

Can I use FCG Grow without any AI provider?

Yes. The Kanban board works fully without an AI provider. You just won't have access to the AI assistant panel until you add one.

Is FCG Grow really free?

Yes. The app is completely free and open source. The only potential cost is if you choose a paid AI provider -- but free options (OpenRouter, Groq, Ollama) work great for most tasks.

How do I get FCG Grow as a standalone app without installing Python?

Download the FCGGrow.exe file directly from wherever you got this app. Double-click to run -- no Python, no installation, no terminal required. If you only have the .pyw file, contact the person who shared it with you and ask for the .exe version.

The titlebar on Settings and task dialogs flashes white before turning dark.

This is fixed. All windows (including Settings, Edit Task, and the main window) are hidden before the dark chrome flag is applied, then shown — so they are dark from the very first frame. If you are still seeing a flash on an older machine, try closing and reopening the dialog once to let Windows catch up.
